Welcome to Srivani English Medium School

We are committed to building a safe, engaging and inspiring learning environment — one where students grow strong academic foundations alongside confidence, creativity and responsible values. Our teachers know every child by name, and our classrooms are built for curiosity, not just curriculum.

From the first day of school to the final board exam, our aim stays the same: help each student discover what they're capable of.

About Our School

A brief look at who we are, how we teach, and why families choose us.

School Introduction

Located in Nereducherla, Telangana, Srivani English Medium School offers English-medium education from the early years through secondary school, with a focus on both academic rigour and all-round development.

Our Approach

We combine structured academics with activity-based learning, encouraging students to ask questions, work in teams and apply what they learn — not just memorise it.

Why Parents Choose Us

Small class sizes, attentive teachers, a safe campus and consistent communication with families give parents confidence in their child's daily learning experience.
